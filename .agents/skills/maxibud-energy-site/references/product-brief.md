# MAXIBUD ENERGY Product Brief

## 1. Project Summary

MAXIBUD ENERGY is a fictional product line created for an educational Nuxt website project.

The website is associated with MAXIBUD LLC / ТОВ «МАКСІБУД», but the product line, products, specifications, visuals, documents, and technical claims are conceptual unless explicitly marked otherwise.

The software project demonstrates:

- product information architecture;
- content-driven product pages;
- bilingual UX;
- responsive design;
- technical specification presentation;
- comparison;
- consultation workflow;
- SEO-friendly public pages.

It is not proof of real manufacturing or product availability.

## 2. Problem Statement

A company with several related technical products needs a separate product-oriented website that:

- explains the product portfolio more clearly than a general corporate site;
- supports technical product storytelling;
- helps users identify a suitable product;
- centralizes specifications and documents;
- captures consultation requests;
- works in Ukrainian and English.

A plain one-page business card would be too weak. A full online store would be unnecessary and dishonest because there is no real commerce scope.

## 3. Product Vision

Create a focused product website where a visitor can move from:

1. use context;
2. product recommendation;
3. product details;
4. comparison;
5. consultation request.

The experience should be simple enough for a non-technical homeowner but detailed enough for a contractor or business buyer.

## 4. Business Objective

Primary objective:

- present a coherent fictional product portfolio and generate structured demonstration inquiries.

Secondary objectives:

- create reusable product-page architecture;
- demonstrate Nuxt 4 and content-driven development;
- support SEO and bilingual presentation;
- provide material for an academic practice report.

The website must not optimize for direct sales because pricing, availability, and fulfillment are outside the project.

## 5. Target Audiences

### 5.1 Homeowner

Needs:

- backup for essential home systems;
- understandable capacity and power information;
- quiet and compact concept;
- comparison against portable options;
- consultation before choosing.

Questions:

- Which product is intended for a house?
- Can capacity expand?
- What systems is it designed to support?
- Does it work with solar and a hybrid inverter concept?
- How much space does it require?

Recommended product:

- HomeCore 5.

### 5.2 Mobile contractor or service team

Needs:

- portable power for tools and field tasks;
- quick understanding of output and charging;
- manageable weight;
- rugged transport concept;
- temporary backup.

Questions:

- Can it power common tools?
- How heavy is it?
- Can it recharge from solar or a vehicle?
- Is it suitable for rain or dust exposure?

Recommended product:

- PowerBox 2400.

### 5.3 Construction site manager

Needs:

- higher output;
- robust enclosure;
- transport and service access;
- emergency control;
- structured technical discussion.

Questions:

- Is it intended for professional loads?
- Can it support several tools or temporary infrastructure?
- How is it moved?
- Is remote monitoring part of the concept?
- What input options exist?

Recommended product:

- SiteHub 10.

### 5.4 Small business or office

Needs:

- continuity for essential equipment;
- compact stationary installation;
- predictable expansion;
- product documentation;
- consultation.

Recommended product:

- HomeCore 5, with SiteHub 10 for temporary or higher-output use.

### 5.5 Distributor or partner

Needs:

- complete portfolio view;
- consistent technical presentation;
- bilingual material;
- structured inquiry;
- shareable URLs.

No fake distributor program terms should be shown.

## 6. Jobs to Be Done

### Visitor

- When grid power is unstable, help me understand which concept system matches my environment.
- When I compare products, show meaningful differences without forcing me to decode technical tables.
- When I need more information, let me submit a structured consultation request.
- When I share a product, provide a stable localized URL with complete metadata.

### Company representative

- When product content changes, let me update structured content without rewriting page templates.
- When a new product is added later, reuse the same page architecture.
- When reviewing inquiries, receive consistent product and inquiry-type fields.
- When presenting the project academically, demonstrate more than a static landing page.

## 7. Brand Positioning

### Positioning statement

For homeowners, contractors, and construction teams who need clear information about backup and mobile power concepts, MAXIBUD ENERGY presents a focused portfolio of practical energy systems for home, work, and site environments.

### Value pillars

1. **Practical use**
   - products map to specific environments;
   - information prioritizes decisions over slogans.

2. **Clear technical information**
   - specifications are grouped and readable;
   - product differences are explicit.

3. **Scalable portfolio**
   - portable, modular stationary, and professional site categories;
   - shared design language.

4. **Responsible communication**
   - conceptual status is disclosed;
   - no unsupported certification or safety claim.

## 8. Brand Voice

Use:

- direct sentences;
- concrete benefits;
- restrained technical language;
- short paragraphs;
- descriptive headings;
- clear calls to action.

Avoid:

- “revolutionary”;
- “industry-leading”;
- “unmatched”;
- “guaranteed safety”;
- “zero risk”;
- “military grade”;
- “green future” without evidence;
- “trusted by thousands”;
- fear-based messaging about outages.

## 9. Product Portfolio Logic

### PowerBox 2400

The accessible first product.

It is:

- portable;
- lower capacity;
- aimed at mobile work and short-term backup;
- visually compact;
- the easiest product to understand.

### HomeCore 5

The central portfolio product.

It is:

- stationary;
- modular;
- intended for homes and small businesses;
- expandable;
- visually architectural rather than tool-like.

### SiteHub 10

The professional top-tier product.

It is:

- mobile but heavy;
- high-output;
- rugged;
- intended for construction and temporary infrastructure;
- visually industrial.

## 10. Success Criteria

A successful visitor should be able to:

- name the three product categories after viewing the homepage;
- choose a likely product within one minute;
- find capacity and rated output without searching through prose;
- understand that specifications are conceptual;
- switch language without losing the current product;
- submit a consultation request;
- compare products on mobile.

## 11. Product Risks

### Risk: concept presented as real

Mitigation:

- footer disclosure;
- legal page;
- specification disclaimer;
- no price, stock, certification, or commercial availability.

### Risk: technical overload

Mitigation:

- key highlights before full tables;
- grouped specifications;
- plain-language applications;
- use-case selector.

### Risk: generic template appearance

Mitigation:

- original product visuals;
- strict design system;
- product-specific sections;
- limited card usage;
- original copy.

### Risk: false social proof

Mitigation:

- omit testimonials and partner logos;
- use product facts and project logic instead.

### Risk: unnecessary project scope

Mitigation:

- no checkout;
- no accounts;
- no CMS admin;
- no live hardware data;
- no complex calculator.
