# Copy and Localization

## 1. Copy Principles

Write for comprehension.

Preferred sentence style:

- direct;
- concrete;
- moderate length;
- one claim per sentence;
- technical term followed by plain meaning where useful.

Avoid:

- exaggerated adjectives;
- vague sustainability claims;
- fear-based outage messaging;
- unsupported safety language;
- “smart” as a substitute for explaining behavior;
- literal machine translation;
- repeated “innovative solution” language.

## 12. Validation Copy

Use specific field messages.

Examples:

| Condition | Ukrainian | English |
|---|---|---|
| required | Заповніть це поле | Complete this field |
| invalidEmail | Вкажіть коректну електронну адресу | Enter a valid email address |
| shortName | Ім’я має містити щонайменше 2 символи | Name must contain at least 2 characters |
| shortMessage | Опишіть запит щонайменше 20 символами | Describe the request in at least 20 characters |
| longMessage | Повідомлення не може перевищувати 3000 символів | Message cannot exceed 3000 characters |
| consent | Потрібна згода з описаною обробкою даних | Agreement to the described data handling is required |

Avoid “Something went wrong” when a more specific message exists.

## 15. SEO Copy Guidance

Product title pattern:

UK:

> {Product name} — концептуальна система живлення | MAXIBUD ENERGY

EN:

> {Product name} concept power system | MAXIBUD ENERGY

Descriptions must:

- include product context;
- remain under reasonable search-snippet length;
- state “concept” or “концептуальна”;
- avoid certification and availability language;
- differ by product.

## 16. Translation Review

Before completion:

- compare Ukrainian and English section by section;
- verify model names and numbers;
- verify decimal formatting;
- verify units;
- verify CTA intent;
- verify legal meaning;
- verify success-message truth;
- remove literal word-order artifacts;
- test long Ukrainian labels in narrow layouts.
